01 / A little about me

A head for numbers.
A heart for people.

I grew up in the central region of Sri Lanka, a beautiful island that will always be part of who I am. My professional journey began at KPMG, where working across different industries gave me a foundation in audit, accounting, and the discipline of looking closely at the details. That foundation took me into finance roles in the UAE, Seychelles, Maldives, and New Zealand. Working in a regulated financial environment in Dubai was an important turning point. Each country brought different people, expectations, and ways of working — and another opportunity to learn. Today, Auckland is home. I’m a husband, a proud father, and someone who values kindness and a can-do attitude. I enjoy meeting people, adapting to change, and finding useful ways to contribute. For me, a good career and a meaningful life grow together.
